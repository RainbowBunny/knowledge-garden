## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $M$: a [[knowledge/math/set theory/Set\|Set]].
> - $\circ$: a [[knowledge/math/set theory/operation/Binary Operation\|Binary Operation]] on $M$.

> [!definition] Magma
> Requires:: [[knowledge/math/set theory/operation/Binary Operation\|Binary Operation]].
>
> ---
> A **magma** is a set $M$ together with a binary operation $\circ$ that satisfies [[knowledge/math/property/operation/Closure\|Closure]]. `[AAN]` `[BS81 II §1]`

> [!remark] Name
> `[BS81 II §1]` calls a magma a *groupoid* (an algebra with just one binary operation); this is unrelated to the category-theoretic [[knowledge/math/algebra/category/example/Groupoid\|Groupoid]].
