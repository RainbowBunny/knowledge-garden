## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $n \geq 3$: an integer.

> [!definition] Generalized Quaternion Group
> Instantiates:: [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/Group\|Group]].
>
> ---
> The **generalized quaternion group** $Q_{2^n}$, of order $2^n$, is generated by $x$ and $y$ with
> $$x^{2^{n-1}} = e, \qquad y^2 = x^{2^{n-2}}, \qquad yxy^{-1} = x^{-1}.$$
>
> `[ConGQ Thm 3.2]`. `[ConGQ Def 3.1]` constructs it as the quotient of $\mathbb Z/(2^{n-1}) \rtimes \mathbb Z/(4)$ by the subgroup generated by $(2^{n-2}, 2)$.

> [!definition] Quaternion Group
> $Q_8 = Q_{2^3}$: generated by $i$ and $j$ with $i^2 = j^2 = -1$ and $jij^{-1} = -i$, where $-1$ is central of order 2. Its elements are $\pm 1, \pm i, \pm j, \pm k$ with $k = ij$, units of the quaternions.
>
> `[ConGQ §2]`

## Property

> [!proposition] One element of order 2, and it is the center
> **Scope**
> - $n \geq 3$.
>
> ---
> **Property**
> - The only element of order 2 in $Q_{2^n}$ is $x^{2^{n-2}} = y^2$.
> - $Z(Q_{2^n}) = \{e, x^{2^{n-2}}\}$.
>
> `[ConGQ Cor 4.3, Thm 4.2(2)]`

> [!theorem] Characterization — the $p$-groups with one subgroup of order $p$
> **Scope**
> - $G$: a finite [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/special group/p-Group\|p-Group]].
>
> ---
> **Property**
> The following are equivalent:
> - $G$ has a unique subgroup of order $p$;
> - every abelian subgroup of $G$ is cyclic;
> - $G$ is cyclic or generalized quaternion.
>
> `[ConGQ Thm 4.10]`. For 2-groups of order at least 4: $G$ is cyclic iff it has exactly one subgroup of order 2 and exactly one of order 4 `[ConGQ Cor 4.11]`.

## Example

> [!example] Order 8 and 16
> - $Q_8$ is one of the two nonabelian groups of order 8, with $D_4$ ([[Groups of Order p³\|Groups of Order p³]]); $Q_8$ has one element of order 2, $D_4$ has five.
> - $Q_{16}$ is the extension type $(C_8, 2, x \mapsto x^{-1}, x^4)$ ([[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/extension/Cyclic Extension\|Cyclic Extension]], [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/classification/Groups of Order 16\|Groups of Order 16]]).
