## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $n \geq 3$: an integer.

> [!definition] Dihedral Group
> Instantiates:: [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/Group\|Group]].
>
> ---
> The **dihedral group** $D_n$ is the group of rigid motions taking a regular $n$-gon to itself, under composition. It has $2n$ elements: $n$ rotations and $n$ reflections.
>
> `[ConDih §1, Thm 2.5]`

> [!remark] Two names
> Conrad writes $D_n$ for the group of order $2n$, and notes that group theorists write $D_{2n}$ for it `[ConDih §1]`. The vault follows $D_n$: $D_4$ has order 8 and $D_8$ order 16.

## Property

> [!proposition] Presentation — $r^n = s^2 = e$, $srs^{-1} = r^{-1}$
> **Scope**
> - $r$: a rotation by $2\pi/n$; $s$: a reflection.
>
> ---
> **Property**
> $D_n$ is generated by $r$ and $s$ with
> $$r^n = e, \qquad s^2 = e, \qquad srs^{-1} = r^{-1},$$
> and its elements are $r^i$ and $r^i s$, $0 \leq i < n$.
>
> `[ConDih Thm 3.1]` ([[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/Group Presentation\|Group Presentation]])

> [!proposition] Center — trivial for odd $n$, $\{e, r^{n/2}\}$ for even $n$
> **Scope**
> - $n \geq 3$.
>
> ---
> **Property**
> $$Z(D_n) = \begin{cases} \{e\}, & n \text{ odd}, \\ \{e, r^{n/2}\}, & n \text{ even}. \end{cases}$$
>
> `[ConDih Thm 3.4]` ([[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/subgroup/Group Center\|Group Center]])

> [!proposition] Elements of order 2
> **Scope**
> - $n \geq 3$.
>
> ---
> **Property**
> The $n$ reflections $r^i s$ have order 2. The only rotation of order 2 is $r^{n/2}$, for even $n$. So $D_n$ has $n$ elements of order 2 for odd $n$, and $n + 1$ for even $n$.
>
> `[ConDih §1]` describes the $n$ reflections for odd and even $n$; $(r^i s)^2 = r^i (s r^i s^{-1}) = r^i r^{-i} = e$.

> [!proposition] A semidirect product — $D_n \cong C_n \rtimes C_2$
> **Scope**
> - $C_n = \langle r \rangle$, $C_2 = \langle s \rangle$, $s$ acting on $C_n$ by inversion.
>
> ---
> **Property**
> $$D_n \cong C_n \rtimes C_2.$$
>
> `[ConSD]` ([[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/extension/Semidirect Product\|Semidirect Product]])

## Example

> [!example] Small dihedral groups
> - $D_3 \cong S_3$, of order 6, the nonabelian group of order $2 \cdot 3$ ([[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/classification/Groups of Order pq\|Groups of Order pq]]).
> - $D_4$, of order 8, one of the two nonabelian groups of order 8 ([[Groups of Order p³\|Groups of Order p³]]).
> - $D_{15}$, of order 30, has 15 elements of order 2 ([[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/classification/Groups of Order 30\|Groups of Order 30]]).
