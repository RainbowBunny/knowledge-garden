## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/Group\|Group]]: Import $G$.
> - $A$: a [[knowledge/math/set theory/Set\|Set]] (the generators).
> - $F(A)$: the [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/special group/Free Group\|Free Group]] on $A$.
> - $R$: the normal subgroup of $F(A)$ generated by the relations.

> [!definition] Group Presentation
> Requires:: [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/special group/Free Group\|Free Group]].
>
> ---
> A **presentation** of a group $G$ is an explicit [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/homomorphism/Group Isomorphism\|Isomorphism]]
> $$G \cong \frac{F(A)}{R}$$
> where $A$ is a set, $F(A)$ is the [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/special group/Free Group\|Free Group]] and $R$ is the [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/subgroup/Normal Subgroup\|Normal Subgroup]] of $F(A)$ generated by a set of 'relations'; one writes $(A \mid R)$. `[Alu II.8.2]` `[Gar21 Def I.9.4]`
