## Definition

> [!definition] Parameters
> - $G$: a [[knowledge/math/set theory/Set\|Set]].
> - $\circ$: a [[knowledge/math/set theory/operation/Binary Operation\|Binary Operation]] on $G$.

> [!definition] Semigroup
> Extends:: [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/Magma\|Magma]].
>
> ---
> A **semigroup** is a set $G$ together with a binary operation $\circ$ that satisfies [[knowledge/math/property/operation/Closure\|Closure]], [[knowledge/math/property/operation/Associativity\|Associativity]]. `[AAN]` `[BS81 II §1]`

> [!definition] Semigroup (Alternative)
> Alternatively, a **semigroup** is a [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/Magma\|Magma]] whose binary operation $\circ$ satisfies [[knowledge/math/property/operation/Associativity\|Associativity]]. `[AAN]` `[BS81 II §1]`
