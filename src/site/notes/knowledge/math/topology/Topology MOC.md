# Topology MOC

Index for `math/topology/`. The notes follow Munkres, *Topology*, Chapter 2, so far §§12–19, with Hatcher's, Morris's and Møller's notes as second sources.

> [!intuition] Why this field
> Topology keeps only the idea of nearness, which points are close to which, and drops the distances. That is enough to define continuity, convergence and connectedness once, for metric spaces, ordered sets, $p$-adic numbers and the Zariski topology alike. In this vault it is the language behind the Borel σ-algebra and the $p$-adic numbers.
