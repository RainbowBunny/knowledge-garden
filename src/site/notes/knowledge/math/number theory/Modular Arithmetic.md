## Definition

> [!definition] Congruence
> Instantiates:: [[knowledge/math/set theory/relation/Equivalence Relation\|Equivalence Relation]]
> 
> ---
> Let $n$ be a positive integer. Consider the relation on $\mathbb{Z}$ defined by
> $$(\forall a, b \in \mathbb{Z}): a \equiv b \bmod n \iff n \mid (b - a).$$
> This is called **congruence module** $n$.

> [!remark]
> The set of equivalence classes is often denoted by $\mathbb{Z}_n, \mathbb{Z}/(n), \mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z}$, or $\mathbb{F}_n$. The most common one is $\mathbb{Z}_n$.

> [!proposition] 
> Let $m \geq 1$ be an integer.
>- If $a_1 \equiv a_2 \pmod m$ and $b_1 \equiv b_2 \pmod m$, then $$a_1 \pm b_1 \equiv a_2 \pm b_2 \pmod m \quad \land \quad a_1 \cdot b_1 \equiv a_2 \cdot b_2 \pmod m.$$
>- Let $a$ be an integer. Then $$\exists b: a \cdot b \equiv 1 \pmod m \iff \gcd(a, m) = 1.$$If such an integer $b$ exists, then we say that $b$ is the (multiplicative) inverse of a modulo $m.$

> [!definition] Integers Module $n$
> Instantiates:: [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/ring/special ring/Quotient Ring\|Quotient Ring]] $\mathbb{Z}$ by the ideal $(n)$.
> Instantiates:: [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/ring/special ring/Commutative Ring\|Commutative Ring]].
> Instantiates:: [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/special group/Cyclic Group\|Cyclic Group]] $\mathbb{Z}/n$, generated by 1.

## Property

> [!proposition]
> - $(\mathbb{Z}_n, +)$ is a [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/Group\|Group]].
> - Multiplication makes $\mathbb{Z} / n \mathbb{Z}^* = \{[m]_n \in \mathbb{Z} / n \mathbb{Z} \mid \mathsf{gcd}(m, n) = 1\}$ a [[knowledge/math/algebra/structures/group/Group\|Group]].
