## Definition

> [!definition] Closure
> ### Scope
> A [[knowledge/math/set theory/operation/Binary Operation\|Binary Operation]] $\star: O \times O \rightarrow O$ and a subset $A \subseteq O$
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> An operation $\star$ is closed in $A$ iff 
> $$\forall x, y \in A: x \star y \in A$$
