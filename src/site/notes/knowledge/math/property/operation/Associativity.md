## Definition

> [!definition] Associativity
> ### Scope
> A [[knowledge/math/set theory/operation/Binary Operation\|Binary Operation]] $*: O \times O \rightarrow O$.
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> An operation $*$ is associative iff 
> $$\forall x, y, z \in O: (x * y) * z = x * (y * z)$$
