## Definition

> [!definition] Commutativity
> ### Scope
> A [[knowledge/math/set theory/operation/Binary Operation\|Binary Operation]] $*: O \times O \rightarrow O$.
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> An operation $*$ is commutative iff 
> $$\forall x, y \in O: x * y = y * x$$
