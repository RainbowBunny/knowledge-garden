## Definition

> [!definition] Identity Element
> ### Scope
> A [[knowledge/math/set theory/operation/Binary Operation\|Binary Operation]] $*: O \times O \rightarrow O$.
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> An operation $*$ has an identity element iff 
> $$\exists e \in O \; \forall x \in O: x * e = e * x = x$$
