## Definition

> [!definition] Distributivity
> ### Scope
> Two [[knowledge/math/set theory/operation/Binary Operation\|Binary Operation]] $\oplus: O \times O \rightarrow O, \otimes: O \times O \rightarrow O$.
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> Two binary operations $\oplus, \otimes$ are distributive iff for all $x, y, z \in O$:
> - $x \otimes (y \oplus z) = (x \otimes y) \oplus (x \otimes z)$ (Left Distributivity).
> - $(x \oplus y) \otimes z = (x \otimes z) \oplus (y \otimes z)$ (Right Distributivity).
