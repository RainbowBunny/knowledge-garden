## Definition

> [!definition] Symmetry (Relation)
> ### Scope
> A relation $\sim \; \subseteq O \times O$.
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> A relation $\sim$ is symmetric iff 
> $$\forall x, y \in O: x \sim y \Longrightarrow y \sim x$$
