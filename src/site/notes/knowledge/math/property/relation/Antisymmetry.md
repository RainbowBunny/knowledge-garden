## Definition

> [!definition] Antisymmetry
> ### Scope
> A relation $R \subseteq O \times O$.
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> A relation $R$ is antisymmetric iff 
> $$\forall x, y \in O: (x \;R\; y) \land (y \;R\; x) \Rightarrow x = y$$
