## Definition

> [!definition] Connexity (Totality)
> ### Scope
> A relation $R \subseteq O \times O$.
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> A relation $R$ is connex iff 
> $$\forall x, y \in O: (x \;R\; y) \lor (y \;R\; x)$$
