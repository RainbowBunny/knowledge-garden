## Definition

> [!definition] Transitivity
> ### Scope
> A relation $\sim \; \subseteq O \times O$.
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> A relation $\sim$ is transitive iff 
> $$\forall x, y, z \in O: x \sim y \land y \sim z \Longrightarrow x \sim z$$
