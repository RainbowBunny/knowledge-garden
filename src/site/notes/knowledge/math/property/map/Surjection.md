## Definition

> [!definition] Surjection (onto)
> ### Scope
> A [[knowledge/math/set theory/Function\|Function]] $f: A \rightarrow B$
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> $f$ is **surjective** if 
> $$\mathsf{ran}(f) = B$$
> We denoted as $f: A \twoheadrightarrow B$.
