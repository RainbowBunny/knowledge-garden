## Definition

> [!definition] Injection (one-to-one)
> ### Scope
> A [[knowledge/math/set theory/Function\|Function]] $f: A \rightarrow B$
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> $f$ is **injective** if 
> $$\forall a_1, a_2 \in A: f(a_1) = f(a_2) \implies a_1 = a_2$$
> We denoted as $f: A \hookrightarrow B$.
