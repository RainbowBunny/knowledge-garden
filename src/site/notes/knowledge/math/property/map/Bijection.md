## Definition

> [!definition] Bijection (one-to-one correspondence)
> ### Scope
> A [[knowledge/math/set theory/Function\|Function]] $f: A \rightarrow B$
> 
> ---
> ### Property
> $f$ is **bijective** if $f$ is both an [[knowledge/math/property/map/Injection\|Injection]] and a [[knowledge/math/property/map/Surjection\|Surjection]],
> We denoted as $f: A \stackrel{\sim}{\rightarrow} B$ and also write $A \cong B$. We say that $A$ and $B$ are 'isomorphic' sets.
