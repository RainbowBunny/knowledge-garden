## Definition

> [!definition] Equivalence Relation
> A [[knowledge/math/set theory/relation/Relation#Definition\|Relation]] is an equivalence relation iff it satisfies [[knowledge/math/property/relation/Reflexivity\|Reflexivity]], [[knowledge/math/property/relation/Symmetry\|Symmetry]], [[knowledge/math/property/relation/Transitivity\|Transitivity]].

### Equivalence Class 

> [!definition] Equivalence Class
> For every element $a \in S$, the **equivalence class** of $a$ (w.r.t. $\sim$) is the subset of $S$ defined by
> $$[a]_\sim = \{b \in S \; | \; b \sim a\}$$
