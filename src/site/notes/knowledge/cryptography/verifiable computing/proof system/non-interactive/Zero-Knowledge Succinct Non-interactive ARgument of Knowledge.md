> A **zero-knowledge SNARK** is a [[knowledge/cryptography/verifiable computing/proof system/non-interactive/Succinct Non-interactive ARgument of Knowledge\|SNARK]] that is also [[knowledge/cryptography/verifiable computing/property/Zero Knowledge\|Zero Knowledge]]. In terms of the base syntax: a [[knowledge/cryptography/verifiable computing/proof system/non-interactive/Non-Interactive Proof Systems\|Non-Interactive Proof Systems]] that is complete, knowledge sound, succinct and zero-knowledge.
