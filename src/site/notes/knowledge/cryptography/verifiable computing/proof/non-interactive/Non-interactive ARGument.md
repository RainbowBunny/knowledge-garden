## Syntax

### NARG

> [!definition] NARG
> A NARG is a [[knowledge/cryptography/verifiable computing/proof/non-interactive/Non-Interactive Proof Systems\|Non-Interactive Proof Systems]] that satisfies [[knowledge/cryptography/verifiable computing/proof/properties/Completeness\|Completeness]] and computational [[knowledge/cryptography/verifiable computing/proof/properties/Soundness\|Soundness]].

### SNARG

> [!definition] Succinct Non-Interactive Argument
> A SNARG is a [[knowledge/cryptography/verifiable computing/proof/non-interactive/Non-interactive ARGument#NARG\|#NARG]] with [[knowledge/cryptography/verifiable computing/proof/properties/Succinctness\|Succinctness]].
