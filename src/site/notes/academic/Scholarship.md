## Rules that apply to all
- **One main scholarship only.** Most of these can't be combined. Apply widely, then accept one.
- **Eiffel:** only one French university can put you forward.
- **IELTS expires in March 2027.** Submit everything due before then with the current score. Ask whether a later enrolment or visa step needs a valid certificate.
- **Campus France / Études en France:** start early. Sophie Germain needs the file number, and Paris-Saclay and Paris Cité admissions probably go through it.

## PSL PhD Track
- **Link:** [PSL PhD Tracks](https://psl.eu/en/education/find-your-curriculum/psl-phd-tracks)
- **Track:** Computer Science, with the second year in MPRI
- **Funding:** €10,000/year for 2 years (€1,000/month × 10 months), then a 3-year doctoral contract (≥ €2,300 gross/month). Awarded with admission; no separate scholarship form.
- **Deadline:** 14/12/2026, 23:59 CET (window 14/10–14/12/2026, PSL portal only)
- **Interviews:** January–March 2027, remote, in English. **Results:** February–April 2027.
- **Documents:**
  - Academic CV
  - Transcripts and diplomas, **merged into one PDF**
  - Statement of purpose (strict 1-page limit)
  - Research project (1–2 pages): past research, current state, planned work if admitted
  - English proof at C1 level
  - Contact details for at least two academic referees
- **Notes:** Eiffel would replace this grant, not add to it.

## ENS International Selection
- **Link:** [ENS International Selection](https://ens.psl.eu/en/node/1291/international-selection) · [FAQ](https://www.ens.psl.eu/en/academics/admissions/international-selection/faq-about-international-selection)
- **Subject:** Computer Science (leads to the ENS computer science department, then MPRI)
- **Funding:** €1,000/month for 36 months, plus a room on an ENS campus
- **Deadline:** 15/12/2026, 14:00 Paris time (20:00 in Vietnam), on the OnePSL30 platform
- **Calendar:** shortlist from 27/01/2027, oral tests 03–19/03/2027 by video call, results from 26/03/2027
- **Documents (one combined PDF):**
  - High school diploma and university transcript
  - CV
  - Cover letter, 1–3 pages (Times New Roman 12, 1.5 spacing) with a study plan
  - 2–4 recommendation letters, requested through the platform
- **Selection:** scientific oral test (weight 6) and an interview, often on a scientific article (weight 3). In English or French. Admission rate is 5–10%.
- **To check:** can someone who already holds a bachelor's degree apply? Ask ENS before investing time.

## IP Paris PhD Track
- **Link:** [PhD Track in Computer Science](https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/education/phd-track/phd-track-computer-science) · [Admissions](https://www.ip-paris.fr/en/education/admissions)
- **Majors to list:** Foundations of Computer Science (MPRI-related) and Cybersecurity. You can put up to 5 programmes in one application.
- **Funding:** €10,000/year for the 2 master's years, then 3 years of doctoral funding. Considered automatically with admission.
- **Deadline:** 14/01/2027 (end of session 1). The track's own 2027 dates aren't posted yet. Last cycle it closed 20/01/2026.
- **Results:** within 2 months of the session closing (about mid-March).
- **Documents:**
  - Transcript and CV
  - Statement of purpose
  - Two academic referees, who submit through the portal
  - English at B2 (TOEIC, IELTS, TOEFL or Cambridge)
- **Notes:** apply on the IP Paris portal, not Campus France.

## PGSM Master
- **Link:** [PGSM Master](https://sciencesmaths-paris.fr/en/our-programs/master/pgsm-master) · [Application form](https://applications.sciencesmaths-paris.fr/en/login)
- **Courses (Paris Cité):**
  1. M1 Parisian Research Master in Computer Science (M1_UPC_MPRI): **first choice**
  2. M1 Mathematics and Computer Science for Cryptology (M1_UPC_MIC): second choice, if the form allows two. Probably taught in French.
- **Funding:** €1,150/month, for one or two years
- **Deadline:** 22/01/2027 (first call, international, opened 30/10/2026). References are due 31/01/2027, according to the application portal.
- **Results:** first half of March 2027
- **Documents:**
  - CV
  - Cover letter (1 page maximum)
  - Transcripts and bachelor's degree
  - Master's transcripts and diploma, only if you have them
  - 1–3 recommendation letters
- **Notes:** the second call (26/02–20/04/2027) is only for students already in the FSMP network. Apply separately to the master's at Paris Cité: the scholarship doesn't replace admission.

## ENS Lyon scholarships
- **Link:** [ENS Lyon master's admissions and scholarships](https://www.ens-lyon.fr/en/node/366815)
- **Application:** one form for the master's and the scholarship. **Opens 30/11/2026, closes 22/01/2027 at 23:59 CET. Results mid-March 2027.**
- **Scholarships:**
  - **Ampère:** €1,000/month × 12 (2025–26 rules). Foreign nationals only, with an age limit. Can't be combined with other funding. The internship must be in an ENS Lyon lab, and no study abroad is allowed.
  - **Graduate+ (MathInfI):** €1,000/month × 10. Can be combined with other funding.
  - **QuantEduFrance:** €1,000/month × 12, for fundamental computer science students who commit to a quantum-sciences internship at ENS Lyon.
- **Renewal for the second year (Ampère and Graduate+):** not automatic. You must pass the first year and be selected again.
- **Notes:** fallback route. Contact the ENS Lyon referee.

## Sophie Germain
- **Link:** [Apply for a Sophie Germain scholarship](https://fondation-hadamard.fr/en/our-programs/transversal-programs/graduate-program/apply-for-a-sophie-germain-scholarship/) · [Eligible master's](https://fondation-hadamard.fr/en/articles/2025/09/30/list-of-eligible-masters-degrees/)
- **Funding:** €11,000 net/year. Also a registration-fee allowance, up to €2,000 for travel and visa, a studio in Orsay or Évry, and French courses.
- **Deadline:** 24/01/2027, 23:59 Paris time (first call, for students outside Paris-Saclay). References due 31/01/2027. Results about 5 weeks later.
- **Eligible master's:**
  - M1 Applied Algebra and Cryptology (UVSQ / Versailles): **best crypto fit, taught in French**
  - M2 Applied Algebra and Cryptology (Paris-Saclay)
  - M1 Jacques Hadamard Track, École Polytechnique ([international](https://fondation-hadamard.fr/en/campus-paris-saclay/training/master/jacques-hadamard-international-program/)): in English, pure maths
  - M1 Jacques Hadamard Track, ENS Paris-Saclay
- **Documents:**
  - Personal information
  - Name of the master's programme
  - Study plan (max 2 pages): who you are, interests, goals, anything unusual in your CV or transcripts
  - Transcripts and academic CV
  - Names and emails of two referees
  - Campus France file number (non-European applicants)
- **Notes:** doesn't cover MPRI. Ask the first-year lead, Balthazar Bauer, whether an IT degree is accepted. Can't be combined with other excellence scholarships.

## Eiffel via Paris-Saclay
- **Link:** [Paris-Saclay Eiffel page](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/en/admission/bourses-et-aides-financieres/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship)
- **Funding:** €1,200/month, for 24 months if you start in M1. Also travel, insurance and housing help. Tuition is not covered.
- **Deadline:** contact the master's programme coordinator **before 26/11/2026** (MPRI M1, e.g. the ENS Paris-Saclay track)
- **Calendar:** internal decision by 07/12/2026. The university sends files to Campus France around early January. Regular master's application from February 2027. National results around mid-April 2027.
- **Eligibility:** born after 31/03/1997, first Eiffel application, not previously studied in France, one university only
- **Notes:** this is your single Eiffel nomination. Use it here rather than at PSL, where it would replace the PhD Track grant.

## Paris-Saclay IDEX scholarship
- **Link:** [International Master's Scholarships Program](https://www.universite-paris-saclay.fr/en/admission/bourses-et-aides-financieres/international-masters-scholarships-program)
- **Funding:** €10,000/year, plus up to €900/year for travel and visa
- **Expected deadline:** around 25–31/03/2027. In 2026 the coordinator selection closed 25/03, the form and references 31/03, and results came mid-May.
- **Eligibility:**
  - You must already be **admitted** to a Paris-Saclay master's
  - Under 30
  - First enrolment in French higher education
  - **Not eligible if you receive more than €600/month from other funding**, so it rules out holding Eiffel too
- **Notes:** backup if Eiffel fails.

## Erasmus Mundus CYBERSURE
- **Link:** [How to apply](https://cybersure-master.eu/admission/how-to-apply)
- **Structure:** Year 1 at NTNU (Norway). Year 2 specialization, including **Cryptography at the University of Tartu (Estonia)**.
- **Funding:** Erasmus Mundus scholarship (tuition, travel and a monthly allowance). The amount isn't on the page; check the scholarships page.
- **Deadline:** 04/01/2027, 23:59 (GMT+1). Window opened 16/11/2026. English test results must arrive by 18/01/2027.
- **Documents:**
  - Bachelor's certificate and transcripts (with certified English translations)
  - Motivation letter (max 500 words)
  - **Motivation video** (max 1 minute)
  - CV, passport and proof of residence
  - IELTS (minimum 6.5 overall, 6.0 writing)
  - Two academic letters (recommended)
- **Notes:** a master's only, not a PhD route. It's more applied security than theory.

## Hong Kong
**Deadline: 01/12/2026.** Apply to the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship Scheme (HKPFS, deadline 12:00 noon Hong Kong time) and to each university.

### PolyU: Presidential PhD Fellowship Scheme (PPPFS)
- **Link:** [PPPFS](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/gs/prospective-students/fellowship-scholarship-schemes/pppfs/)
- **Funding:**
  - HK$340,800/year stipend
  - HK$25,000 conference grant
  - Cash award of HK$40,000 in year 1, HK$20,000/year after
  - Tuition waiver
  - Hall place for one year
- **How:** HKPFS applicants are considered for PPPFS at the same time. There's no separate form.

### HKU: Presidential PhD Scholar Programme
- **Link:** [HKU Presidential PhD Scholar Programme](https://gradsch.hku.hk/prospective_students/fees_scholarships_and_financial_support/hku_presidential_phd_scholar_programme)
- **Funding:** up to ~HK$445,800 in year 1. That's HK$28,700/month, plus a cash award, HK$14,400/year for travel, a composition-fee waiver and a hall place.
- **How:** every full-time PhD applicant in the main round (01/09–01/12) is considered. HKU strongly encourages also applying for HKPFS.

## SMU Vingroup Young Talent
- **Link:** [Vingroup Young Talent Scholarship](https://graduatestudies.smu.edu.sg/phd/vingroup-young-talent-scholarship)
- **Funding:** full tuition plus a monthly stipend (amount not stated)
- **Deadline:** 31/01/2027 for the PhD in Computer Science, August 2027 intake (window opened 01/08/2026)
- **Eligibility:** of Vietnamese origin, admitted to an eligible SMU programme (PhD in Computer Science included)
- **Obligation:** **return to Vietnam within 2 years** of finishing the PhD, and work there for as many years as you were funded
- **Notes:** SMU is not strong in cryptography theory. Check that an advisor fits before applying.
